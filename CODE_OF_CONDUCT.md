# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Mermaidman is an open-source engineering project. We want participation to be technically rigorous, respectful, and welcoming to people with different levels of experience and different points of view.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy project include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people;
- explaining tradeoffs and assumptions;
- crediting other people's work;
- helping contributors reproduce and understand problems;
- respecting privacy and confidential security reports;
- accepting maintainer decisions when a discussion has reached a conclusion.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or discriminatory abuse;
- personal insults or sustained hostility;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberately disrupting issues, reviews, or community spaces;
- using security disclosures to threaten, extort, or embarrass contributors.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-managed communication spaces, and when someone is officially representing Mermaidman.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

For sensitive conduct concerns, do not create a public issue. Contact the repository maintainer through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Include enough context for the concern to be evaluated, but avoid sharing unrelated personal information.

Maintainers should handle reports as privately as practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately to the behavior and its impact.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is informed by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant, while keeping the reporting process specific to this repository.
